april / story.md

I’m April (Hạnh in Vietnamese), a doer and a marketer. I was born in Spring, but I’ve spent my life chasing Winter. As a tropical girl from Vietnam, my biggest dream has always been to study and experience life in places with a real winter. In my work, it means the same thing: starting from the warmth of marketing craft, and chasing the crisp, clear logic of systems and technology.

I work to solve business problems through marketing, product, and analysis. By day, I’m a 9-to-5 operator who puts business impact first. I help the companies I work for grow, and I get paid for the value I bring. Simple, honest, win-win.

I love the 90-9-1 rule: out of 100 people, 90 consume, 9 distribute, and 1 builds. I’m still a complete newbie on the builder path, taking baby steps to move from consuming to distributing to building tiny projects of my own, like Snapcard, Marketing Systems Lab, and this website.

I hold an M.S. in Marketing Management, though my real school has always been YouTube, AI, the internet, and life itself. I come from a non-tech, no-code background and only recently became active on GitHub after AI opened the door to technology for me. Honestly, everything about it still makes me go “wow.”

I enjoy learning anything that helps me make money honestly and helps the people I collaborate with grow.
