the craft · 2026-10-28 · 4 min read

why i’m moving from marketing craft into marketing systems

plenty of people can pull a number. fewer can say what marketing should do because of it.

i started my career in brand and product marketing. craft is where i come from: understanding customers, shaping positioning, and telling a clear story.

but the deeper i went into growth across hospitality, fintech, marketplaces, and global saas, the more i realized that craft alone hits a ceiling if it is not backed by systems.

craft vs. systems

  • craft is knowing what message resonates with a customer.
  • systems is building the tracking plan, kpi tree, funnel diagnosis, and automation loop so that every campaign compounds over time.

plenty of people can pull a number from a dashboard. fewer can say what marketing should actually do because of that number, and what trade-offs that decision creates for the business.

what i am practicing now

  1. measurement & analytics: building kpi trees and diagnosing funnel drop-offs before spending a dollar on ads.
  2. ai fluency: turning repetitive marketing tasks into reusable, tested ai agent skills.
  3. marketing automation: connecting data, lifecycle triggers, and attribution into one clean engine.

born in spring (craft), chasing winter (systems). that is the whole thesis of my work right now.
